Selling it on owner financing

The buyer owns the home, makes payments to you, and pays the lot rent and repairs. You hold the title until it's paid off. Check your state's rules (some treat this as a loan).

Usually the buyer pays lot rent, insurance and tax, so "costs you still pay" is $0.

Will people stop here?

Score a piece of land before you buy it. Look up traffic counts on your state DOT's traffic map, and check flood zones at FEMA's flood map.

What travelers want: easy in and out, pull-through sites at least 70 ft long for big rigs, 50-amp power, Wi-Fi that works, a clean bathhouse, a dog area, late self check-in, and good reviews on Google, Campendium and RV LIFE.
